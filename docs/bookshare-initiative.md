## The idea (as published on the site)

A catalogue of books their owners are willing to lend, connected through a
network for borrowing and returning them. Book clubs, reviews, and a
platform for young Gambian writers can develop out of it.

## Funding & governance model (not yet published)

BookShare is funded by an **annual membership fee**. Fees are pooled and
released to the network **quarterly**. Each quarter, members **vote on
which books to buy** with the pooled funds. **Limitless Africs manages the
fund and the resulting book collection** on the network's behalf — it holds
custody, handles purchasing, and keeps the catalogue.

Alongside membership dues, there are two other ways money can enter the
system:
- A general **donation to the network**, added to the pooled fund.
- A **sponsored purchase of a specific book** for the network, bypassing
  the quarterly vote — the donor picks the book directly.

Musa gave an illustrative example while explaining this: *"if 20 people
contribute 200 per quarter, that's two books that can be bought."* **This
number was explicitly a placeholder to illustrate the mechanism, not a
real fee, currency, or book price** — when this becomes a real page, the
actual membership fee, currency, cadence, and cost-per-book need to be
decided (and confirmed) before publishing anything with real numbers.

- How the physical book network works operationally — where books are

  this needs its own small system or a manual/community-run process
  initially.
- Relationship to the book clubs / young-Gambian-writers / reviews angle
